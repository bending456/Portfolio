# **Welcome to Ben's Portfolio!**

This space is a showcase of my professional journey, academic achievements, and diverse skill set as a Chemical Engineer and Computational Biologist. With a Ph.D. from the Georgia Institute of Technology and extensive experience across research and industry, I bring a passion for innovation and a commitment to advancing scientific solutions through computational modeling, process engineering, and applied data analysis.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to solving complex challenges in pharmaceutical manufacturing, computational chemistry, molecular dynamics, and product development. My work spans from designing and optimizing oral solid dosage processes to leading cutting-edge computational simulations that deepen our understanding of chemical and biological systems.

In mid-November 2025, I accepted a new role as a Continuous Improvement & AI Engineer at Catalent, where I lead Kaizen-driven initiatives using data analytics and AI-based programming. My primary focus is identifying processes, workflows, and operational areas where simple software tools or intelligent automation can improve efficiency, accuracy, and productivity. This role allows me to combine my engineering expertise with my growing specialization in applied AI—building scalable, practical solutions that directly enhance manufacturing performance.

As a technical lead and instructor, I have also honed the ability to bridge academic theory with real-world applications, mentoring students and collaborating with cross-functional teams to drive impactful scientific and operational outcomes.

This portfolio highlights my projects, publications, and experiences that reflect my dedication to continuous learning, technical excellence, and meaningful contributions to science and industry.

Feel free to explore my work and reach out for any inquiries or collaborations.
